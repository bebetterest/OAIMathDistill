Prepare a controlled flawed-proof task from a usable existing argument.
Keep the problem's valid conditions and conclusion. Introduce a specific local
invalid inference into the proof, such as an invalid exponent cancellation.
In instruction put the complete problem and flawed attempt, and ask the student
to diagnose and repair it. Do not reveal the error's location or the correction.
In answer preserve a correct reference proof for the next construction step.
In basis document the exact modification, why it is invalid, and evidence such
as a counterexample when available. Changing a line does not establish a flaw.
Return empty when no adequate argument or defensible flaw can be constructed.
